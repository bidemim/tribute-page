Pandemic Heroes

Celebrating Nigerian health workers who stopped the spread of the Ebola Virus in

At the heart of the fight against Ebola in Nigeria was Dr Ameyo Stella Adadevoh.
  • Dr Adadevoh diagnosed American-Liberian Patrick Sawyer with Ebola when he was hospitalised in Lagos.
  • The doctor and her staff physically intervened when Mr Sawyer tried to leave the treatment centre.
  • This action cost Dr Adadevoh and three medical staff their lives when they too contracted the disease.
